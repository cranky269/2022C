# 2022 Asia and Pacific Mathematical Contest in Modeling — Problem C
# 2022 年亚太地区大学生数学建模竞赛 —— C 题

## Global Warming OR Not?
## 全球变暖，还是不是？

---

### 第 1 页 / Page 1

Canada's 49.6 °C has set a new temperature record for regions above 50 °N of the earth, and hundreds of people died of heat within a week; Death Valley, California, USA, is 54.4 °C, which is the highest temperature ever recorded on the earth; Kuwait 53.5 °C, even more than 70 °C under direct sunlight, and more than 50 °C in many countries in the Middle East.

> 加拿大的 49.6 °C 刷新了地球北纬 50° 以北地区的气温纪录，一周之内数百人因高温死亡；美国加利福尼亚州死亡谷达到 54.4 °C，是地球上有记录以来的最高气温；科威特为 53.5 °C，在阳光直射下甚至超过 70 °C，中东许多国家的气温也超过 50 °C。

**Figure 1. The high temperature in North American.**

> **图 1. 北美地区的高温。**

Since this year, we have seen a large amount of amazing temperature reports. The reality that the earth is burning is beyond doubt. Following the terrible high temperature in these regions from the end of June to the beginning of July, Italy once again set a European temperature record, reaching an astonishing 48.8 °C, and many countries declared a state of emergency.

> 今年以来，我们看到了大量令人震惊的气温报道。地球正在"燃烧"这一事实已毋庸置疑。在 6 月底至 7 月初这些地区出现可怕的极端高温之后，意大利再次刷新了欧洲气温纪录，达到惊人的 48.8 °C，多个国家宣布进入紧急状态。

Global climate warming is a phenomenon related to nature. It is due to the continuous accumulation of greenhouse effect, which leads to the imbalance of energy absorbed and emitted by the earth atmosphere system, and the continuous accumulation of energy in the earth atmosphere system, leading to temperature rise and global climate warming.

> 全球气候变暖是一种与自然相关的现象。它源于温室效应的不断累积，导致地球大气系统吸收与发射的能量失衡，能量在地球大气系统中持续累积，从而引起气温上升与全球气候变暖。

Prior to the Industrial Revolution, carbon dioxide (CO2) in the atmosphere was consistently around 280 parts per million (ppm). The concentration of CO2 in the atmosphere reached 377.7 ppm in March of 2004, resulting in the largest 10-year average increase up to that time.[1] According to scientists from National Oceanographic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA) and Scripps Institution of Oceanography (SIO) the monthly mean CO2 concentration level peaked at 421 ppm in May 2022.[2] An Organisation for Economic Co-Operations and Development (OECD) report predicts a CO2 level of 685 ppm by 2050.[3]

> 工业革命之前，大气中的二氧化碳（CO2）含量一直稳定在约 280 ppm（百万分之一）。2004 年 3 月，大气中 CO2 浓度达到 377.7 ppm，构成了截至当时最大的 10 年平均增幅。[1] 据美国国家海洋和大气管理局（NOAA）与斯克里普斯海洋研究所（SIO）的科学家称，2022 年 5 月 CO2 月均浓度峰值达到 421 ppm。[2] 经济合作与发展组织（OECD）的一份报告预测，到 2050 年 CO2 水平将达到 685 ppm。[3]

The APMCM organizing committee has asked your team to address these claims of the current reported and future predictions of global temperature levels. They provided data set 2022_APMCM_C_Data.csv which contains 239177 records to assist in your research.

> 亚太杯数学建模竞赛（APMCM）组委会要求你们团队针对目前所报道的以及未来预测的全球气温水平的相关论断作出回应。组委会提供了包含 239177 条记录的数据集 2022_APMCM_C_Data.csv 以协助你们的研究。

### 第 2 页 / Page 2

## Requirements
## 任务要求

**1. Do you agree with the claims of global temperature? Use 2022_APMCM_C_Data.csv in the attachment and other datasets your team collects to analyze global temperature changes.**

> **1. 你是否认同关于全球气温的这些论断？请使用附件中的 2022_APMCM_C_Data.csv 以及你们团队收集的其他数据集，分析全球气温变化。**

a) Do you agree that the increase of global temperature in March 2022 resulted in a larger increase than observed over any previous 10-year period? Why or why not?

> a) 你是否认同"2022 年 3 月全球气温的上升幅度，比此前任何 10 年期间所观测到的增幅都更大"这一说法？请说明理由。

b) Based on the historical data, please build two or more mathematical models to describe the past and predict the future global temperature level.

> b) 基于历史数据，请建立两个或两个以上的数学模型，用以描述过去的并预测未来的全球气温水平。

c) Use each of your models in 1(b) to predict global temperatures in 2050 and 2100, respectively. Do any of your models agree with the prediction that the average global temperature of observation points in 2050 or 2100 will reach 20.00 °C? If not in 2050 or 2100, when will the average temperature of observation points in your prediction models reach 20.00 °C?

> c) 使用 1(b) 中的每一个模型，分别预测 2050 年和 2100 年的全球气温。你的模型中是否有一个与"2050 年或 2100 年观测点的平均全球气温将达到 20.00 °C"这一预测相一致？如果到 2050 年或 2100 年时尚未达到，那么在你的预测模型中，观测点的平均气温将在何时达到 20.00 °C？

d) Which model you built in 1(b) do you consider most accurate? Why?

> d) 你认为你在 1(b) 中建立的哪个模型最准确？为什么？

**2. What are the reasons that affect the temperature change?**

> **2. 影响气温变化的原因有哪些？**

a) Use the results of question 1 and the data in the attachment 2022_APMCM_C_Data.csv and other datasets collected by your team, build a mathematical model to analyze the relationship (if any) between global temperature, time and location, and explain the relationship or prove that there is no relationship between them.

> a) 利用问题 1 的结果、附件 2022_APMCM_C_Data.csv 中的数据以及你们团队收集的其他数据集，建立一个数学模型来分析全球气温、时间与地理位置之间的关系（如果存在的话），并解释该关系，或证明它们之间不存在关系。

b) Please collect relevant data and analyze the factors of natural disasters (such as volcanic eruptions, forest fires and the COVID-19). Is there any impact on global temperature?

> b) 请收集相关数据并分析自然灾害因素（如火山喷发、森林火灾和新冠肺炎疫情）的影响。它们是否对全球气温产生了影响？

c) What do you think is the main reason that affects the global temperature change?

> c) 你认为影响全球气温变化的主要原因是什么？

d) Do you think there are some measures to curb or slow down global warming?

> d) 你认为是否存在一些遏制或减缓全球变暖的措施？

**3. Prepare a non-technical article (1 page maximum) Please write a non-technical article (1 page at most) to the APMCM organizing committee, explaining your team's findings and suggestions for the future.**

> **3. 撰写一篇非技术性文章（最多 1 页）。请向 APMCM 组委会撰写一篇非技术性文章（最多 1 页），说明你们团队的发现以及对未来的建议。**

Your PDF solution of no more than 25 total pages should include:
- One-page Summary Sheet.
- Table of Contents.
- Your complete solution.
- One-page non-technical Article.

> 你们的 PDF 解答（总页数不超过 25 页）应包含：
> - 一页摘要页（Summary Sheet）。
> - 目录（Table of Contents）。
> - 完整的解答过程。
> - 一页非技术性文章。

Note: The APMCM Contest has a 25-page limit. All aspects of your submission count toward the 25-page limit (Summary Sheet, Table of Contents, Article). However, the pages of Reference List and Appendices are not limited.

> 注：APMCM 竞赛有 25 页的页数限制。提交作品的所有部分均计入这 25 页限制之内（包括摘要页、目录、文章）。但是，参考文献列表和附录的页数不受限制。
